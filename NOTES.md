- **Help → Mount Workspace…** shows how your own computer, Mac, Windows or Linux, mounts the Workspace as a drive, with the address and the password ready to copy, and **New Password** there too.
- The Workspace is a volume on the Mac: **Go → Connect to Server…** in Mac OS X, with the address and password under **Mount Workspace** in the Mac OS X window's **Setup…** menu.
- On a phone, a key row under the terminal windows sends **Esc**, **Tab**, **Shift-Tab** and the arrows.
