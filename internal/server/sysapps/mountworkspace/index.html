The Workspace mounts on your own computer as a drive named Workspace. What you drop into it is on this desk at once, and what the desk or an agent saves there is on your computer.

every computer that mounted signs in again
  1. In the Finder choose Go → Connect to Server… (⌘ K).
  2. Enter the address and click Connect.
  3. Sign in with the name and the password. The Finder can keep them in your keychain.

Workspace then stands on your desktop and in the Finder’s sidebar. Eject it to unmount.

To have it back after a restart, add Workspace to your Login Items in System Settings → General, or keep the address in Connect to Server’s list with its + button.

  1. In File Explorer open This PC and choose Map network drive (on Windows 11, under the toolbar’s … button).
  2. Pick a drive letter, enter the address as the folder, tick Connect using different credentials, and click Finish.
  3. Sign in with the name and the password.

Windows takes a password over plain http only after the first line below; an https address needs no change. The second line lets files over 50 MB through. Run them in an administrator Command Prompt, then restart the computer.
