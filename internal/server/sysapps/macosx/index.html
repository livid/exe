Install Mac OS X — follow these steps in the Mac screen
  1. Choose your language and click the arrow. When Install Mac OS X appears, choose Utilities → Disk Utility… from the Mac’s own menu bar.
  2. In Disk Utility select 100.0 GB QEMU HARDDISK, click the Erase tab, give the disk a name, click Erase and confirm. Then choose Disk Utility → Quit Disk Utility.
  3. In the Installer click Continue, agree to the license, select your new disk, click Continue, then Install. You may Skip the check of the DVD.
  4. Copying takes a quarter of an hour or more. At its end the Installer restarts the Mac and this screen closes: click Installation finished — start my Mac below.

Disk Utility’s Erase applies to this Mac’s virtual hard disk. Use it only during this first installation.

Your Mac is shut down. Its files are saved. Click Start Mac to return to the desktop.

Setup details

Setup runs on this computer and continues if you close this dialog. Your Mac’s hard disk is kept for your next visit. Installer source.

While the Mac runs, this computer and no other reaches two of its Sharing services: Remote Login on port — and Apple Remote Desktop’s VNC on port —, both at 127.0.0.1. Turn them on in the Mac’s System Preferences → Sharing.

In the Mac’s Finder choose Go → Connect to Server…, enter the address, click Connect, and sign in with the name and password.

The Agent button opens a Chat that works inside the Mac over Remote Login, as the Mac account . Once, let its key in: paste this line into Terminal on the Mac, signed in as that account.

Tiger’s Remote Login must be on, and the account is any the Mac’s Accounts pane lists; its short name is what the Terminal’s prompt shows.
